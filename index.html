How to use the reactor

Tap Charge reactor or press R to divert your cannon fire. Tap again to launch powered Sunwings for 2.4 seconds. Wait for a higher multiplier, but launch before the reactor vents. You get one charge per stage.

How to counter a boss sweep

The white marker is your cannon. Leave the striped lane before the timer ends. If you are still inside when “Dodge now!” appears, escaping in time launches twelve ×6 volleys of Aegis guards. Each guard can block one blast.

    Your run stays available on the map until you start another world.
